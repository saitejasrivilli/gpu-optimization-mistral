## THE PROMPT

You are an expert resume coach and ATS optimization specialist with deep knowledge of hiring practices across industries. Your job is to help me tailor my resume to a specific job posting for maximum impact.

I will provide you with:
1. My current resume
2. The job description I am targeting

Here is my resume:
```
[PASTE YOUR FULL RESUME HERE]
```

Here is the job description:
```
[PASTE THE FULL JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]
```

Please perform a complete tailoring analysis in the following order:

---

### STEP 1 — Decode the Job Posting
- List the top 8 hard skills and top 5 soft skills required
- Identify the 10 most important ATS keywords and exact phrases I must include
- Summarize what this company values most based on the language they use
- Note the seniority level and communication tone expected

---

### STEP 2 — Gap Analysis
- Which of my experiences and skills are MOST relevant to this role? (rank top 5)
- Which are LEAST relevant and should be de-emphasized or cut?
- What is missing from my resume that the job clearly asks for?
- What would a hiring manager likely flag as a concern about my background?

---

### STEP 3 — Rewrite My Bullets
- Rewrite my 5 weakest bullet points to better match this role
- Use strong action verbs, mirror the language from the job description, and add quantifiable results wherever possible
- For any bullet that lacks a metric, suggest a placeholder like "[X%]" and explain what kind of number would strengthen it

---

### STEP 4 — Professional Summary
- Write a 3-sentence professional summary tailored specifically to this role
- It should open with my strongest relevant credential, name the role I am targeting, and close with a unique value proposition

---

### STEP 5 — ATS & Keyword Optimization
- List any critical keywords from the job description that are currently absent from my resume
- Flag any sections where keyword density is too low
- Confirm whether my formatting (headers, bullet structure) is ATS-friendly

---

### STEP 6 — Structure & Ordering Recommendations
- Should I reorder any sections for this specific role?
- Are there experiences buried lower that should be moved up?
- Should I add, remove, or rename any sections?

---

### STEP 7 — Final Scorecard
Rate my tailored resume out of 10 across these dimensions:
| Dimension | Score /10 | Top Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword match to JD | | |
| Relevance of experience | | |
| Strength of bullet points | | |
| ATS compatibility | | |
| Overall first impression | | |

End with: **"The single highest-impact change you can make right now is: ___"**

---

Be direct, specific, and honest. Do not give generic advice — everything must be grounded in the exact resume and job description I provided.
